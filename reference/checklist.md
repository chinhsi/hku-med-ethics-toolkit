# Submission checks

Use the actual [FE33/226 amended checklist](current-med-rules.md); this file supplements it.

Compare proposal ↔ summary ↔ instruments ↔ each consent language ↔ FE54/926. Verify titles, dates, people, procedures/duration/occasions, every data source, pilot/main scope, recording choices, retention/destruction, withdrawal limits, storage/access, legal limits, matching and concrete power safeguards. Record exact document/section evidence and outstanding supervisor decisions.

Principal permission, parental permission and participant consent/assent have different roles. Confirm the active/passive parental route using actual official conditions. For video, protect children declining capture; include review/erasure rights for recordings.

Hong Kong forms need the supervisor's reachable telephone. Confirm its owner and reachability, not a student's phone, generic switchboard or copied example. Compare all three languages manually.

No fabricated signatures, approval/reference numbers, prior permissions or completed actions. No example facts or TO CONFIRM text in recruitment copies. Keep approval fields blank in drafts. Render Word files and check Chinese/page breaks; ensure summary is one page.

A script report identifies missing structure, not full ethical compliance. Official checklist Done/N/A requires actual verification. Supervisor/reviewer endorsement is followed by the Office of Research approval email before data collection.
