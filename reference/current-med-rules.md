All Capstone projects need the form and an up-to-date one-page methodology summary, including projects without human participants. A No answer is not a self-issued exemption. Human-participant projects also need applicable English/first-language consent forms and instruments. Checklist items 2–3 require English and original-language interview/questionnaire versions. Cover pilot and main study.

School studies need principal permission even if principals provide no data. Checklist item 6 contains conditional minimal-risk routes: below Primary; Primary with anonymous/non-sensitive data; Secondary; outside-school adolescents aged 16+. Read the exact conditions with the supervisor, including active/passive parent consent; these are not universal opt-out permission. Use age-appropriate assent (kindergarten, lower/upper Primary, lower/upper Secondary); children may refuse independently.

Describe procedures, duration/occasions, location/data, storage method/location/duration, access, confidentiality safeguards/limits, matching, power safeguards. Recordings need review/erasure rights; classroom video needs nonconsent arrangements.

**Checklist item 18 requires a readily reachable supervisor telephone in Hong Kong consent forms. It is not the student's telephone.** Item 19 requires English/Chinese consistency.

A generic “Part E” reference in the checklist does not create Part E in FE54/926. Discuss applicable sensitive-conduct issues with the supervisor/reviewer and add a clearly labelled supplementary statement if required.

Student reads/confirms the declaration; supervisor endorses; designated reviewer requests changes/approves/signs; full signed pack goes to Office of Research (currently Ms Wing Pau, swing.pau@hku.hk). Data collection starts only after its approval email. The declaration also prohibits project data collection/analysis before required approval. Never invent signatures/reference numbers/permission.
