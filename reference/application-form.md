# Application route

Use [current MEd rules](current-med-rules.md) and [fields](fields.md). The upstream general HREC Part A–E guidance is superseded in this derivative by Capstone FE54/926 Part A–C. Dissertation uses a separate official form.

The generator makes Part A field/summary drafts and an evidence tracker; with an official DOCX copy it fills Part A only. It never signs declarations, fills Parts B/C, submits to HREAS or emails the Office of Research.
